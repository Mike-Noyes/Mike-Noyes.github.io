## Quick Introduction

We will continue the study of *Integration* during this section of the course.  All of the usual notions that you are familiar with from previous material reappear here:

* Antiderivatives
* Substitution
* Integration by Parts
* Numerical Integration

While they are all similar to the familiar ideas from the previous material, they take on a new and richer meaning in the context of improper integrals and applications.  In general, the key difference is that we are now looking at more complex scenarios.

For instance, in the previous section we saw that certain techniques work for specific types of functions.  Now we see that sometimes we need to combine multiple techniques or use more advanced methods.
