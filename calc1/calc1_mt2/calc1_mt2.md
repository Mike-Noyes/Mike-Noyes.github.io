## Quick Introduction

We will begin the proper study of *Derivatives* during this section of the course.  All of the usual notions that you are familiar with from Calc 1 appear here:

* Limits
* Continuity
* Rates of Change
* Applications

While they are all familiar from the previous material, they take on new and richer meaning in the study of derivatives.  In general, the key difference is that we are now looking at how functions change.

For instance, in the previous section we saw that a limit exists if and only if the left-hand and right-hand limits exist and are equal.  Now we use limits to define the derivative as a special kind of limit.
