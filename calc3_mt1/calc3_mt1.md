## Introduction

Most of the material on this midterm is *Geometric*.  We will be focusing on vectors, in either 2 or 3-dimensional space: $\vec{v}=\langle v_1, v_2 \rangle$ or $\vec{v}=\langle v_1, v_2, v_3 \rangle$.

The two most important operations on vectors are the *dot product* and the *cross product*.  Please note that while the dot product is defined for any two vectors of the same size, the cross product is only defined for two 3-dimensional vectors.

The dot product of the two vectors $\vec{v} = \langle v_1, v_2, v_3 \rangle$ and $\vec{w} = \langle w_1, w_2, w_3 \rangle$ is

$$\vec{v}\cdot \vec{w} = v_1w_1 + v_2w_2 + v_3w_3$$

The most basic objects that we use are lines (which are special *curves*) and planes (which are special *surfaces*).

Given a point $P(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ in space and a direction vector $\vec{v}$, the parametric equations for the line through $P$ in the direction of $\vec{v}=\langle v_1, v_2, v_3 \rangle$ is given by

$$\vec{r}(t) = \vec{r}_0 + t\vec{v} = \langle x_0 +v_1t, yo + v_2t, z_0+v_3t \rangle$$
