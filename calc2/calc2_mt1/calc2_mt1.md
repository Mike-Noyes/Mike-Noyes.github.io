## Quick Introduction

Most of the material on this midterm is about *Integration*.  We will be focusing on finding antiderivatives and using them to solve problems.  Integration is the **natural** inverse operation to differentiation.

The two most important techniques for integration are *substitution* and *integration by parts*.  Please note that not all integrals have closed-form solutions, and sometimes we need to use numerical methods.

After basic integration, the most important application we use is finding areas and volumes.  These are fundamental geometric problems that can be solved using integration.

Given any function, it is extremely useful to be able to *integrate* it in different ways.  **PLEASE NOTE** that integration techniques vary depending on the form of the function.
