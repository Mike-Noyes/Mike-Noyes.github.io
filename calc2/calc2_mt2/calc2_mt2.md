# This is the homepage for the Calc 2 Midterm 2 page.

## Quick Introduction

We will continue the study of *Integration* during this section of the course. All of the usual notions that you are familiar with from previous material reappear here:

* Antiderivatives
* Substitution
* Integration by Parts
* Numerical Integration

While they are all similar to the familiar ideas from the previous material, they take on a new and richer meaning in the context of improper integrals and applications.
