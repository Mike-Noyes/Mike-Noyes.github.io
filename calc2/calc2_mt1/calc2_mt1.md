# This is the homepage for the Calc 2 Midterm 1 material.

## Quick Introduction

Most of the material on this midterm is about *Techniques of Integration* and related applications. We will focus on understanding how to construct and interpret antiderivatives and definite integrals.

The goal is to build fluency in solving problems that involve substitution, integration by parts, and applications that connect mathematics to modeling.
