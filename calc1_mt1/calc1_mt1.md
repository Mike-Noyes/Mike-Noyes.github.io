## Quick Introduction

Most of the material on this midterm is *Algebraic*.  We will be focusing on the fundamental properties of functions and limits.  Functions are the **natural** way to work with relationships between quantities.

The two most important operations on functions are *composition* and *inversion*.  Please note that not all functions are invertible, and composing functions requires careful attention to domains and ranges.

After functions, the most basic objects we study are limits, which allow us to analyze the behavior of functions near specific points.  This is the foundation for all of calculus.

Given any function, it is extremely useful to be able to *analyze* it in different ways.  **PLEASE NOTE** that analyzing a function graphically, numerically, and algebraically are different but complementary approaches.
